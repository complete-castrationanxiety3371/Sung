## 👋 You're Ready to Listen!

Once Sung opens, you'll see a friendly welcome screen. Here's how to start playing music right away:

- **To play your own music:** Click the **"Library"** tab on the left side. Then click the **"Add Folder"** button and choose the folder where your music is stored. Sung will scan it and show all your songs. Click any song to play it.


- **To stream from YouTube Music:** Click the **"Search"** tab at the top. Type the name of a song or artist you like, press Enter, and you'll see results. Click one to start playing instantly.



You can adjust the volume, skip tracks, pause, and seek through a song using the controls at the bottom of the window, just like you would on any music app.
